📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Amazon product sales performance. The dashboard provides insights into Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales, Quarterly Sales, Product Performance, and Customer Reviews, enabling business stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Objectives

Track overall sales performance (YTD & QTD).

Visualize sales trends by month and week.

Compare sales across product categories.

Identify top-performing products by sales and customer reviews.

Provide interactive filters (Product Category & Quarter) for deeper analysis.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop – Dashboard creation & visualization.

Amazon Sales Dataset (sample dataset).

Data Transformation – Power Query Editor.

DAX Measures – For custom KPIs and calculations.

📈 Dashboard Features
🔹 KPIs

$2.18M YTD Sales

$811.09K QTD Sales

27.75K Products Sold

19.42M Customer Reviews

🔹 Visual Insights

Sales by Month → Line chart showing seasonal patterns.

Sales by Week → Bar chart for weekly performance tracking.

Sales by Product Category → Category-wise breakdown with % contribution.

Top 5 Products by YTD Sales → Identifies highest revenue drivers.

Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews → Shows most popular items among customers.

🔹 Filters

Product Category (e.g., Camera, Men Clothes, Shoes, etc.)
